# The joke moves

> Style reference only. Study the shapes and cadence here, then write them with the nouns, characters, and commands of the game you are actually building. Do not import this game's world.


Every Peasant's Quest response is one of about twenty moves. Pick one per line.
Each entry: the move, how to build it, and canon examples (verbatim from the game).

## 1. Refuse with a physical or bureaucratic excuse
The world says no for a stupid, specific, modern reason.
- "Your butt is too big." (climb in bucket)
- "No go. Your insurance doesn't cover that." (climb in well)
- "There aren't enough footholds and you don't have any carabeaners on you."
- "You can't, it's tied to a rope and you're terrible with knots."
- "\oard of Peasant Health says I can't serve any folk that's on fire. Nothing personal. Well, I mean, I don't like you much anyway."

## 2. Insult the player's competence
- "It's the Kerrek, you moron! Get outta here!"
- "You attempt to cause Poor Gary harm for no good reason other than you kinda suck at this game."
- "If you weren't a simpleton you might think these were Kerrek prints. But since you are a simpleton, you just go, 'Yay! Piggies!'"
- "Dumb, dumb, dumb. Nice one, William Tell."

## 3. Sarcastic nickname (vocative snark)
Address the player as a famous person or an invented loser.
- Swimmer Dan · Mispeller Jones · Boring Sanders · Mr. Smarty-short-pants · Cupid ·
  Kid Icarus · MJ · Father of the Year · Trogdorkilla · Dragonheart · Matthew
  Broderick · Dirk the Daring · My dear Adso · fancypants · short pants · guy · pal

## 4. Anachronism inside the medieval frame
- "It's locked. With one o' them SCHLAGE deadbolts, too. Those things are hard core."
- "Night falls like a bad power point presentation"
- "Hmmmm... A baby, eh? You check your sundial. Babies-R-Us is probably closed by now."
- "What a dump! Only one bed and no minibar?" / "But do they have a free breakfast buffet?"
- "It also protects against harmful UV rays." (TrogHelmet)

## 5. Invented pathetic backstory
- "You used to eat this stuff like crazy when you were a kid and your parents weren't watching (which was all too often)."
- "Your dad liked buckets. Meemaw loved cranks."
- "They're mysterious and out of reach. Like girls were to you in high school."
- "You always wanted one for your dorm room but your roommate was a total drag and said no."
- "You didn't get that merit badge in outdoor survival eating strange berries at the drop of a hat."

## 6. Fourth wall — the machine
- "If you want to search more press 'reload' on the browser"
- "No, no. The broom is in the foreground and you can't take things from the foreground. You don't see me all typing 'get browser window', do you?"
- "I think you misread the walkthrough on GameFAQs. You gotta get the baby first."
- "What'd you read that in the PQ Stragedy Guide?"
- "Well, according to these graphics, I'd say the tree was 2 years old."
- "...cleans, guts, and filets all the fish and a buncha other stuff we didn't animate."
- "It was loaned out to the Fishing Challenge videogame."
- "Smells like a computer game."

## 7. Fourth wall — the genre
- "Since this is an adventure game we're gonna proffer you adventure yourself on in there."
- "For what? Now you're just making up puzzles to solve."
- "Right, right. Wouldn't want to leave the robes out where any old guy trying to beat an adventure game could take them."
- "Yecch. I wouldn't touch that unless it was, like, an important quest item or something."
- "You have a sneaking suspicion that SOMEONE in this game will need this thing."
- "Not in this game, Swimmer Dan! In this game, you drown."

## 8. Boredom / go-do-the-quest scolding
- "Are you THAT bored? Do some questing already!"
- "You are an incredibly boring person."
- "You're really hurtin' for puzzle solutions, huh?"
- "Let's get moving, here, people!"
- "Not going to dignify that with a response."

## 9. Deadpan atrocity
Narrate something awful in a warm, encouraging tone.
- "Something tells you this is a good idea and you lob the little one into the lake."
- "Okay, Father of the Year. You put a baby in a bucket and it quickly descends deep into the well. Yours is a black, black heart."
- "Deploying Q-Baby..."
- "Canoeing, fishing, stoning heathens. What a Blast!"

## 10. Absurd epilogue
A tiny action spawns a whole life story, then turns sad.
- "He becomes Valedictorian of his graduating class, goes to Scalding Lake State, gets a degree in Advanced Peasantry and lands a job at Thatch-Pro: building better cottages for a better tomorrow. You grow apart and the letters from him become fewer and fewer. He develops a severe mead problem and blames you for never being there."
- "You toss the feed into the lake. You shed a tear for each one. 'Goodbye, Monty' you sniff. 'Bye Delga, Rasputin. Farewell, Combledon!' and so forth."

## 11. Repeat-command annoyance
- "Come now. We've been through this jaunty little bit before."
- "We've already been through this exercise and it went pretty well, ya?"
- "We did that already, and it was sort of humiliating."
- "You done that already. A great time was had by all."
- "A riddle: What can be closed that is already closed? (hint: not this drawer)"

## 12. Flat monosyllabic dismissal
The shortest possible reply is often the funniest.
- "Meh." · "Shut up." · "Tis broked." · "He gone." · "Like how?" · "No. Just no." ·
  "Nope. It's too sheer." · "Scared me." · "I wish I knew." · "NO MAN! JEEZ!" ·
  "Eww. No. Grody. You're grody. No. Eww."

## 13. Narrator changes its mind mid-sentence
- "No. No. No. No. No. Maybe. Wait, No."
- "Riiight, right. Which ones?"
- "Right, right. Wait, what?"
- "Them puppies are stuck in there like a, um, er ... stuck pig."
- "You make sure to get an even mix of sedentary, igneous, and, um, sureptitious rocks."

## 14. Fake-out reveal in two beats
Set up a thrill, then deflate it.
- "Woah! Gold nuggets! Oh wait...This is just chicken feed. Crap."
- "He's an older peasant with big cotton swabs stuffed up his nose. Oh wait. Those are nose hairs. Grody."
- "Upon closer inspection, you discover it's not magical. That's just mist, not magic sparkles or anything."
- "But you think you hear Trogdor whimpering! Aw crap, that's you whimpering."

## 15. Gross-out with class
Filth is described lovingly and never graphically.
- "It reeks of single parent."
- "There are even more questionable stains on that pillow than there are on that rug."
- "Maybe that used to be a magic carpet. But it's sure a cat pee-stained carpet now."
- "the rancid cloud of odor that follows you like a shadow"
- "Shelves and shelves of something called 'Old Man Rub.'" → `get old man rub`: "You disgust me."

## 16. Mock-literary flourish, immediately punctured
- "A light rain heralds the washing free of the Kerrek's grip on the land. You're feeling pretty good, though, so the artless symbolism doesn't bug you."
- "You'd call out her name and let it ring through the empty room, enveloping you in sorrow, but you don't actually know her name."
- "You reach into the closed drawer and nearly break your hand slamming it. Just like a bird on a pane of glass minus the blamelessness."
- "The ages have worn this rock down. It is a rock of ages. Still a-rollin."

## 17. Malaprop / broken grammar as a character trait
- "irridisregardless" · "You no longer has this item." · "Well, you not exactly dead." ·
  "Yeah but like you already map the took, guy!" · "moster maskus" · "Stragedy Guide" ·
  "jorb" · "haldo" · "You've already gotten an heapin' helpin' all up on yo'self."

## 18. NPC dialect comedy
Each NPC has one speech gimmick, reused everywhere:
- **Jhonka** — caveman telegraphese, all caps energy: "Burninator make fire! Barbecue all peasants!" · "Jhonka don't know nothing bout that!" · "I KILL YOU!!"
- **Baby Lady** — self-pity plus greed, flubs her own lie: "Trogband killed my husbdor."
- **Blue Knight** — condescending fitness coach with movie references.
- **Innkeeper** — old-man gross-out non-answers: "I wear my robe hood-down so it's easier to trim my ear hairs."
- **Mendelev & Dongolev** — bickering twins who derail into "Did not!" / "Did so!" / "(You see where this is going.)"
- **Keepers of Trogdor** — bureaucrats who'd rather take a bribe than do their jobs; "High school dropouts, most of them."
- **Trogdor** — robot-voiced, magnanimous, unbothered: "Sup, mortal."

## 19. Bribe-over-puzzle subversion
The intended solution is to skip the quiz by handing over a sandwich, a soda, and
back pills. "Unless you happen to have something to eat on ya. Then we could forego my
question." Reward bribery and shortcuts with the best jokes.

## 20. Sincere kindness at the finish
After 150 points of abuse, the ending is genuinely warm — which is why the abuse
lands. "Congratulations! You've won! No one can kill Trogdor but you came closer than
anybody ever! Way to go!" · "Peasants are bound to talk about you for years and years.
You'll be a legend!" · "Nice work on winning and everything. THE END"

# Allusion catalog (the reference layer)

The game name-drops constantly. Sources: the [Homestar Runner Wiki fun facts](http://www.hrwiki.org/wiki/Peasant's_Quest).

- **Sierra / adventure games:** King's Quest I–IV (three-task structure, ogre→Kerrek,
  Daventry→Peasantry, well to dragon's lair, leprechaun hole→Ned's cottage, knight-guarded
  path, Gwydion's map), The Black Cauldron (art), Space Quest III (loading screen),
  Zork ("light lantern"), Dungeon (three questions), Dragon's Lair ("Dirk the Daring").
- **Nintendo/Atari:** Kid Icarus (bow), Legend of Zelda (belt-over-head pose, save
  screen), Atari 2600 Adventure (angry Kerrek's vibration), James Bond: Everything or
  Nothing ("Deploying Q-Baby...").

  click, floppy "disk 2 into Drive B", copyright 1982, `disk4of12.html`.
- **Music:** Guns N' Roses "Sweet Child o' Mine" ("a warm, safe place where as a child

  "Turn! Turn! Turn!", Louis Jordan "Is You Is or Is You Ain't My Baby".

  Rings ("filthy peasantses"), The Riddler ("Riddle me this"), Warcraft peasant voice
  (outtakes), Fantastic Four (Mr. Fantastic reach).
- **Brands, twisted:** Depeasant undergarments (Depend), Babies-R-Us, Crunch Berries
  (Cap'n Crunch), Mutton-ums (Steak-umms), Plaguezees (cereal), Indenture(tm) dust

  locks, Old Man Rub, Thatch-Pro, Scalding Lake State.
- **Homestar Runner self-reference:** burninated/Trogdor, "ARROWED!!", "Nice jorb",
  "milks meat", Jhonka (from *impression*), Kerrek (from *interview*), Mendelev (from
  *no loafing*), "make friends with Kerrek" and "buy Kerrek a cold one" (from *for
  kids*), Thy Dungeonman callbacks ("Who do you think you are? Some kind of Thy

**How to reuse this layer in your own game:** pick one nostalgic tech era, one music
era, one movie era, and three fake brands, then hit them relentlessly. Specific and
slightly obsolete beats broad and current.
